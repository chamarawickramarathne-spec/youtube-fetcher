1. Title
YouTube Fetcher

2. Short Description
A fast, clean YouTube video downloader for Windows. Paste a link, pick a quality, and download videos as MP4 or audio as MP3 - all from one simple screen.

3. Long Description
YouTube Fetcher is a lightweight desktop app for Windows that makes downloading YouTube videos and audio effortless. Just paste a video link and the app instantly fetches the available qualities. Choose from Best Quality (merged video+audio), 720p, 1080p, or Audio Only (MP3), and the download starts automatically - with live progress, speed, and ETA shown right on the screen.

Built with a clean, dark interface, YouTube Fetcher supports multiple parallel downloads, a persistent download history, a custom save folder, and smart auto-paste from your clipboard. Behind the scenes it uses yt-dlp, so it stays reliable even as YouTube changes. The app also includes a built-in auto-update system, so you always have the latest version with one click.
