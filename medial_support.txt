1. Title
YouTube Fetcher

2. Short Description
A fast, clean YouTube video downloader for Windows. Paste a link, pick a quality, and download videos as MP4 or audio as MP3 - all from one simple screen. Available for both 32-bit and 64-bit Windows.

3. Long Description
YouTube Fetcher is a lightweight desktop app for Windows that makes downloading YouTube videos and audio effortless. Just paste a video link and the app instantly fetches the available qualities. Choose from Best Quality (merged video+audio), 720p, 1080p, or Audio Only (MP3), and the download starts automatically - with live progress, speed, and ETA shown right on the screen.

Built with a clean, dark interface, YouTube Fetcher supports multiple parallel downloads, a persistent download history, a custom save folder, and smart auto-paste from your clipboard. Behind the scenes it uses yt-dlp, so it stays reliable even as YouTube changes. The app also includes a built-in auto-update system - the version is shown right next to the app name and a one-click update button keeps you on the latest release at all times.

Version 2.1.0 adds a major security hardening and a full dual-architecture build. Both 32-bit (x86) and 64-bit (x64) Windows installers are provided, and the update system automatically selects the correct installer for your system. Every update download is verified with a SHA-256 checksum, TLS certificate verification is enforced, YouTube URLs are validated before download, browser cookie retrieval now requires your explicit consent, and file handling has been hardened against path-traversal and data-corruption attacks.

Originally rebuilt in Python for a tiny footprint - the entire installer is just 83-93 MB (down from 450 MB). Same features, same look, dramatically smaller download.
